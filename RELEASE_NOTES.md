Offline place-name packs for the US, Canada, Western Europe, Australia, New Zealand and Japan, one per region
(core and extra parts, and businesses and addresses for full detail; large regions such as England and Texas are
split into sub-regions), with the history of names. packs.json lists every pack with its size, SHA-256 and
release: core and extra are in this release; the names, businesses and addresses of each publish are in a release
of their own, packs-detail-YYYYMMDD.

Each pack is a collective database: its OpenStreetMap layer is (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright); every other layer is under its own
open licence, listed with its attribution in SOURCES.md, LICENSES.md and NOTICE (all attached to this release).
Built by .github/workflows/build-packs.yml from this repository's code (MIT).
